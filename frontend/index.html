      How the numbers work: total price = (product list price − employee discount, if applied) × (1 + the state's approximate combined sales tax rate). Apple's US list price is the same nationwide — the only thing that changes by state is sales tax, so that's what the map is shading.

      Tax rates are approximate combined state + average local sales tax, sourced alongside the rest of the pricing model in apple_price_data.py. Actual city/county add-ons vary within a state — treat this as a planning estimate, not a receipt.

      Static, single-page site — data comes from data.json (generated by the Python module) at load time. Nothing is sent anywhere; everything runs in your browser.
